Soft Washing

That green film down the shaded side of your house is algae, and blasting it with pressure drives water behind the siding. Soft washing removes it properly.

What soft washing actually is

Soft washing uses low pressure — closer to a garden hose than a pressure washer — combined with cleaning solutions that break down the organic growth itself. The solution does the work, not force. That matters, because the green and black staining on siding and roofs is living growth: algae, mildew and lichen. Pressure knocks the surface layer off and it returns in months. Treating it kills it at the root and it stays gone far longer.

Where it's the right method

  • Vinyl, aluminum and composite siding
  • Roof shingles with black streaking or moss
  • Painted wood, trim and soffits
  • Stucco, EIFS and dryvit
  • Screens, gutters and fascia
  • Fences and decks that would splinter under pressure

Why not just use high pressure?

On siding, high pressure forces water up under the laps and into the wall assembly, where it has no fast way out. On shingles it strips the granules that protect the roof, which shortens its life and can void a manufacturer's warranty. On painted wood it lifts the finish. Anywhere the surface is soft, layered or coated, pressure is the wrong tool — which is most of a house above the foundation.

Plants and pets

Cleaning solutions strong enough to kill algae aren't good for landscaping. We pre-wet beds and plantings before we start, keep them wet through the job, and rinse thoroughly afterward. Pets should stay inside while we work and until treated surfaces have been rinsed.

FAQ

Common questions

Will soft washing damage my siding or roof?

No — that's the entire reason the method exists. Pressure is what causes the damage. Soft washing runs at low pressure and lets the cleaning solution do the work, which is why it's the method shingle manufacturers point to for roofs.

How long before the green comes back?

Properly soft washed, considerably longer than a pressure-washed surface, because the growth is killed rather than knocked off. On a shaded north elevation under trees, expect a couple of years. A sunnier wall can go longer.

Do you do roofs?

Yes. Roof cleaning is soft wash only — high pressure on shingles strips protective granules and can void your warranty.
