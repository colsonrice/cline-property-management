Spring & Fall Cleanups

Two days a year that make the other three hundred and sixty-three easier.

Get a Free Estimate (317) 677-4709

Spring cleanup

Winter leaves a property matted down and full of things that blew in. A spring cleanup clears all of it out and sets the baseline so everything after it — mowing, mulching, planting — lands on clean ground.

  • Beds cleared of leaves, sticks and winter debris
  • Matted turf raked out so it can breathe and green up evenly
  • Ornamental grasses and perennials cut back
  • Bed edges re-cut and defined
  • Fence lines and hard-to-reach corners cleared
  • All debris hauled off the property

Fall cleanup

The fall version is the same idea running the other direction — get everything off the property before it sits under snow all winter, matting the turf and rotting into the beds.

  • Final leaf clearing after the drop finishes
  • Beds cleared and cut back for winter
  • Gutters and hard surfaces cleared of leaf litter
  • Final cut at the right height to go into dormancy
  • Everything hauled away, not blown into the tree line

Overgrowth and neglected properties

If a property has been let go for a season or several — fence lines swallowed by weeds, beds gone to brush, an easement nobody's touched — that's a reclaim rather than a cleanup, and we do those too. It's usually a single hard day that gets everything back to a maintainable baseline.

FAQ

Common questions

When should a spring cleanup happen?

As soon as the ground firms up enough to work without tearing the turf — usually mid-March into April around here. Going too early does more damage than waiting a week or two.

Is fall cleanup the same as leaf removal?

They overlap but they're not identical. Leaf removal is the repeated passes through the drop; the fall cleanup is the single comprehensive pass at the end that also handles bed cut-backs, gutters and the final mow. Most properties want both.

Do you haul the debris away?

Yes, everything goes with us. We don't blow debris into a neighbor's yard or pile it in the back of the property.
